👋 Welcome to SignBridgez

Learn how to turn your hand signs into words

🌟 What Is This?

SignBridgez is a sign language translator. You show hand signs to your camera, and the app writes the letters and reads them out loud.

🚀 How to Start

1

Open the SignBridgez page in your browser (Chrome or Edge is best).

2

When asked, click "Allow" so the app can use your camera.

3

Wait until you see the green dot: 🟢 Ready to Translate A-Z.

4

Hold your hand up in front of the camera and make a letter sign (like A, B, or C).

5

Hold the sign steady for about 1 second. The letter appears on screen!

💡 Make sure your room is well lit and your hand is fully visible.

🎬 How It Works

  • 👁️ The camera watches your hand.
  • 🧠 The app guesses which letter you're making.
  • ✍️ If you hold the sign, the letter is added to your sentence.
  • 🔊 The letter is also spoken out loud.

🔘 The Buttons

  • ␣ Space — puts a space between words
  • ⌫ Backspace — deletes the last letter
  • 🗑️ Clear — erases everything
  • 🔊 Read Sentence — reads your whole sentence out loud
  • 📚 Open Sign Database — see a list of all signs
  • 🇲🇾 BIM SignBank — Malaysian Sign Language website
  • 📖 User Manual — this help page

🔊 Sound Options

  • Tap 🔊 Audio: ON to turn sound off or on.
  • Move the Volume slider to make it louder or quieter.
  • Every letter is spoken as you sign it.

✏️ Try This Example

Want to spell the word "HI"? Here's how:

  • Sign the letter H → hold it → H appears
  • Sign the letter I → hold it → I appears
  • You now see HI on screen 🎉

❓ If Something Goes Wrong

  • Camera not working? → Refresh the page and click "Allow" again.
  • Letters not showing? → Move your hand closer and hold it longer.
  • Wrong letter detected? → Straighten your fingers and hold steady.
  • No sound? → Check that Audio is ON and volume is up.
  • Too dark? → Turn on a light near your face.
⚠️ If the problem continues, refresh the page or restart your browser.

💡 Best Tips

  • ✅ Sit in a bright room
  • ✅ Use a plain wall behind you
  • ✅ Keep your hand steady for 1 second
  • ✅ Keep your hand inside the camera view
  • ❌ Don't move too fast
  • ❌ Don't cover your hand with your body
