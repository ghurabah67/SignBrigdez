📖 User Manual

SignBridgez — A-Z Sign Translator & Word Builder

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
  2. Allow camera access when prompted.
  3. Wait for the status badge to show 🟢 Ready to Translate A-Z.
  4. Hold your hand up in front of the camera and start signing!
💡 Use good lighting and keep your hand clearly in frame for best accuracy.

✋ Translating Signs

The app recognizes hand poses and converts them into letters:

  1. Active Pose shows the letter currently detected (e.g. A (95%)).
  2. Hold the pose for ~1.2 seconds to commit the letter to your sentence.
  3. The letter appears automatically in Spelled Sentence.
  4. Each letter is also spoken aloud (if audio is on).
⚠️ If it shows ..., the confidence is too low — adjust your hand.

🔊 Audio Settings

  • Audio ON/OFF button — toggles all sound
  • Volume slider — adjusts volume from 0% to 100%
  • Letters are spoken as they're added
  • Click 🔊 Read Sentence to hear the full sentence
💡 Turn audio OFF if you're practicing silently or in a quiet room.

📚 Using the Sign Database

  1. Click 📚 Open Sign Database on the main page.
  2. Type in the search box to filter signs instantly.
  3. Each entry shows the letter/word, its category, and a description.
  4. Click the 🔊 button to hear the word spoken.
  5. Click ← Back to Translator to return.

🇲🇾 Malaysian Sign Language (BIM)

The BIM SignBank button opens the official Malaysian Sign Language database:

  • Search signs by keyword (Cari Isyarat BIM)
  • Browse by alphabet (Senarai Abjad)
  • Browse by category (Senarai Kategori)
  • View new signs (Isyarat Baru)
💡 BIM SignBank is free and requires no login. It opens in a new tab.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • ✅ Use a plain background (wall, no clutter)
  • ✅ Keep your hand fully visible in frame
  • ✅ Hold each pose steady for 1-2 seconds
  • ✅ Good front lighting (no backlight)
  • ✅ Position camera at chest/face height
  • ❌ Avoid moving too fast between letters
  • ❌ Avoid dark rooms or strong shadows

ℹ️ About

SignBridgez is a browser-based sign language translator and word builder.

  • Powered by MediaPipe Holistic for hand tracking
  • Uses TensorFlow.js for gesture recognition
  • Supports A-Z fingerspelling and word building
  • Links to BIM SignBank for Malaysian Sign Language
